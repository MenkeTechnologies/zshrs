Three arms, same shell binary (zshrs 0.12.49 sha256 dde99902f0fa812d), same
corpus (Y01completion,Y05describe,Y06values = 65 assertions of the dev tree),
each a pair of runs through the same symlink mirror of Test/, Completion/ and
Functions/ -- one with a substituted Completion/Base/Core/_description, one
without.  No upstream file is modified.

| argvbug | line 83 argv+=( h:... ) -> argv=( h:... ), the pre-fix zshrs behaviour | fail=16 pass=49 | fail=49 pass=16 | 33 |

Reading:

* noop moves nothing, so the mirror does not perturb the suite and the two arms
  of every other run are comparable.
* poison breaks 34 of 49 passing assertions, so a substitution really does reach
  the shell under test -- a zero from this mechanism means "no effect", not
  "not wired up".
* argvbug breaks 33.  That is the measured blast radius of the argv+= bug on
  TODAY's shell, over 65 assertions.

The last number is the point.  When argv+= was actually live (binary @06:25),
symptom counting said it explained 50 of 93 failures and the measured flip when
it was fixed was 1 (see yield_argv_append.txt).  Both are correct: at that time
73 of the 93 were already dead on the lc=/rc= bug, so fixing argv+= could not
show up.  Once lc=/rc= was fixed, the same cause is worth 33 assertions in three
files.  A cause's yield is not a property of the cause; it is a property of the
cause AND everything stacked on top of it, which is why it has to be measured
against the shell you are about to fix rather than counted off a stale run.
